# Calgary launch plan: stores first, then customers

Marketplaces fail on the supply side. Customers need real stock to order; stores join if it costs them little effort and shows quick results.

## 1. Build the prospect list (week 0-1)
Record 30-50 African grocers across all regions (West, East, North, Central, Southern African and mixed stores) in **Admin > CRM** with source, contact, regions and next action. Prioritise stores with good foot traffic, regular stock turnover and a decision-maker on site. Aim to cover different cuisines so discovery is not lopsided.

## 2. Pitch (week 1-3): visit in person
Message: *Reach more customers, showcase your African groceries online, manage incoming orders in one place, and grow your business through a marketplace designed around the products your customers love.* Show the vendor portal live on a phone. Be explicit about the commission, that payment fees are ours, and the refund/substitution rules. Never guarantee sales. Log every call/visit in the CRM; move the stage as they progress.

## 3. Pilot cohort (week 3-6): 3-5 stores
- Pick stores in different regions with a reliable manager and a smartphone/tablet at the till.
- Get their catalogue in via CSV template (we help on-site), photograph top sellers, set tax classes **with an accountant's guidance**, verify documents, approve and publish.
- Set the delivery zone to the neighbourhoods around the pilot stores only. Start with 1-3 drivers and manual dispatch.
- Soft launch to a small group (friends, community groups, staff networks). Keep order volume low enough to fix problems by hand.

## 4. Measure weekly (Admin > Reports)
Time from first contact to launch; vendor activation rate (orders per active store); fill rate (unavailable-item refunds); on-time delivery rate; refund and cancellation rates; zero-result searches (what customers want that we do not list); repeat-purchase rate. Do not scale until fill rate and on-time rate are acceptable.

## 5. Customer acquisition (after the pilot is dependable)
- **Local SEO:** region and country pages, product and store pages with canonical URLs; avoid thin duplicate pages (only products with a live offer are indexed by being listed).
- **Communities:** churches, cultural associations, university student unions, WhatsApp/Facebook groups, with each store promoting its own page.
- **Offers:** first-order coupon (`WELCOME10`-style, admin-created), seasonal/festival campaigns, store promotions.
- **Retention:** reorder in one tap, favourites, recipes-to-basket, consented email.
- **Attribution:** product pages record `utm_source`/`utm_campaign` with views; the funnel report shows what converts.

## 6. Retain and expand stores
Monthly settlement call, share their top sellers and unmet searches (aggregated demand), sponsored placements for stores that want more reach (measured clicks only).

## Risks and mitigations
Late or failed deliveries (start small, manual dispatch, exception queue); stock accuracy (reservations, vendor low-stock alerts, CSV sync later); thin margins (watch net marketplace revenue; adjust commission/fees); trust (honest labels, real reviews only, clear refunds).
