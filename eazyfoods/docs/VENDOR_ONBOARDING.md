# Vendor onboarding guide (for store owners and our sales team)

## What a store gets
Reach new customers; list products from a shared catalogue at your own prices; manage orders in one place; run scheduled sales; see simple reports with gross, commission and net kept separate. We do not promise sales volumes or earnings.

## What it costs
A commission on the merchandise value of each order (the current rate is shown in your dashboard and agreement). Payment-processing fees are paid by EAZyfoods. Tax collected is passed through, not counted as your income.

## Steps (about 30-45 minutes)
1. **Create an account** at the site, then choose "Sell on EAZyfoods" and fill in the application: business name, phone, store name, address, postal code, regions you specialise in.
2. **Complete the Setup checklist** in the Vendor portal:
   - Business details: legal name, business/GST number, contact email.
   - Store location, phone and **opening hours**.
   - **Delivery or pickup** (we assign the delivery areas you serve at approval).
   - Upload a **business licence** (and food-premises permit if you have one). These are stored privately and seen only by our verification team.
   - Read and accept the **vendor agreement** (draft: legal review pending).
3. **Submit for review.** A team member verifies your documents and approves the store. Until then your store is not visible to customers.
4. **List products.** Search the shared catalogue and set your price and stock, **or import a CSV** (download the template under Inventory > Import; use "check only" first to see row errors). If a product is missing, suggest it: it appears once approved.
5. **Connect a payout account** (we guide you after approval). Earnings from delivered orders are paid out after a short hold.
6. **Go live.** Keep stock accurate; accept new orders quickly (we escalate after 15 minutes and refund the customer after 45).

## Day-to-day
- New orders appear in **Orders** (with a badge). Accept or reject (rejecting needs a reason). Mark items **unavailable** (the customer is refunded for that item) or **suggest a replacement** (the customer must approve; they never pay extra).
- Mark orders **ready** when packed; delivery is dispatched by EAZyfoods. For pickup orders, mark **collected**.
- Use **Promotions** for scheduled sale prices and **Ads** for sponsored placements (cost per click, capped budget; approval required; shown labelled "Sponsored").
- **Reports** show the settlement: gross sales, commission, refunds charged to you and net, with a CSV export.

## Rules we hold stores to
Accurate descriptions, ingredients and allergen information; honest stock; no unapproved substitutions; compliance with food-safety and licensing rules that apply to your business (your responsibility).
